**Sell gift vouchers with Cargo. Shoppers choose an amount, a design and who it's for. The recipient is emailed an engraved voucher with a printable PDF, and spends it at checkout, all at once or over several orders.**

Gift Vouchers is built for [Cargo](https://builtwithcargo.dev). Voucher products are Cargo products, codes are applied in Cargo's basket and checkout, and refunds follow Cargo's orders.

## Set or chosen amounts

Sell a £25 voucher, or let shoppers pick any amount between your limits, with quick choices.

## Beautiful vouchers

An engraved, banknote-style pattern in your brand colour, different on every voucher. Or upload your own artwork, or keep it plain.

## A choice of designs

Birthday, Christmas, Thank you: each with its own title, colour and engraved motif. Shoppers see a live preview of exactly what they'll send.

## Emailed, with a PDF

The recipient gets their code, your message and a printable voucher, straight away or on a date the shopper chooses.

## Spent at checkout

Vouchers come off the total after tax, like a payment. Use several on one order; whatever's left stays on the voucher. A fully covered order needs no payment. Works with Cargo's prebuilt checkout: add one partial.

## Safe balances

A balance can't go below zero, and the same money can't be spent twice, even in two checkouts at once. Every change is recorded.

## Refunds and cancellations

Refund the voucher part of a Cargo order back onto its vouchers. Cancelled orders get their voucher money back.

## The Control Panel

**Store → Gift Vouchers**: balances, history, what's still owed, search, CSV, creating vouchers by hand, changing balances, and permissions.

## Your templates

Tags that work without JavaScript, partials for Cargo's prebuilt checkout, and views you can replace with your own Antlers.

## For developers

Events, a PHP API and a JSON API.

## Requirements

- Statamic 6.35.1 or later
- **Cargo 1.15 or later**
- PHP 8.3 or later, and a database (SQLite is fine)
